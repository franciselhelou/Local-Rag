Place your own approved, non-sensitive documents in this folder.

Supported formats: .txt, .md, and text-based .pdf.

After adding, editing, removing, or renaming any supported document, open PowerShell in the local_rag folder and run:

python .\offline_rag.py --build-index --offline --documents .\my_documents

Then ask questions using the same document folder:

python .\offline_rag.py --offline --documents .\my_documents --question "Your question here"

Do not place Microsoft Word .doc or .docx files here in this beginner package. Save them as a text-based PDF first.
